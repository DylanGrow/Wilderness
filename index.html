The Survival Mindset

Panic kills more people than the wilderness. Your mind is your most critical tool—or your greatest liability.

The STOP Protocol

Sit/Stop → Think → Observe → Plan. Apply before every decision.

The Rule of 3s

  • 3 minutes without air
  • 3 hours without shelter (harsh conditions)
  • 3 days without water
  • 3 weeks without food

Address top priorities first

Positive Mental Attitude: Break large problems into small, actionable goals. Talk yourself through it. Survivors who stay calm survive.

The 5 C's of Survival

Five hard items that multiply your natural skills. Pack them first.

C1 — Cutting

Full-tang fixed blade (3-5"). Shelter, food prep, fire processing, first aid.

C2 — Combustion

Lighter ×2, ferro rod, waterproof matches. Tinder: char cloth, fatwood, birch bark.

C3 — Cover

Debris hut, lean-to, quinzhee. Insulate from ground. Dead air space = warmth.

C4 — Container

Single-wall metal bottle. Boil water, cook, render fat, carry liquids.

C5 — Cordage & Essential Knots

Shelter Builds: Keep interiors small to trap heat. Debris hut: ridgepole 9ft, 45° ribbing, 2ft thick debris pile, 4in bedding. Quinzhee: 6-8ft snow mound, sinter 2hrs, hollow, ventilate, mark entrance.

Finding & Purifying Water

Dehydration hits fast. 1hr: thirst. 3hrs: headache/fatigue. 6hrs+: impaired judgment. Follow valleys downhill. Watch for green vegetation, converging animal trails, low-flying insects.

Source Safety Ranking

  1. Rainwater (safest)
  2. Spring source (not downstream)
  3. Fast-moving stream above activity
  4. Still pond/lake (highest pathogen risk)

Daily need: 2-4L depending on temp/activity. Never drink untreated still water. Always purify. Layer improvised filters: grass → sand → charcoal → gravel.

Field Navigation Rules

  • Magnetic vs True: Compass points magnetic north. Adjust for local declination.
  • Taking a bearing: Point arrow at target → rotate bezel until needle aligns with orienting arrow → read bearing.
  • Following a bearing: Pick intermediate landmarks. Box around obstacles (90° turns).
  • Sun shadow stick: Mark shadow tip → wait 15min → mark again → line = East/West.
  • Lost? STAY PUT. Signal, shelter, hydrate. Moving expands search area.

Signal mirror: Hold near eye, look at target, adjust until flash hits. Fire signals: 3 in triangle. Green leaves = white smoke (forest). Rubber = black smoke (snow/sky).

Helicopter: Never run toward it. Arms crossed overhead = help needed. Thumbs up = OK. Stay clear of tail rotor.

Wilderness First Aid

Field triage only. Get to professional help ASAP.

Primary Survey (ABCDE)

Airway → Breathing → Circulation/bleeding → Disability → Exposure

Bleeding

  1. Direct pressure (5-10 min)
  2. Pack deep wounds with gauze
  3. Tourniquet: life-threatening limb only. Note time.
  4. Irrigate with clean water

Fractures & Sprains

Splint joint above & below. Open fracture: cover, don't push bone in. RICE for sprains.

Hypothermia

Mild: warm sweet drinks. Mod/Severe: insulate, rewarm core first. Never extremities first (afterdrop risk).

Heat Stroke

Hot/dry skin OR profuse sweating + confusion. Aggressive cooling: wet, fan, ice to neck/groin/armpits. Evacuate.

Snake Bite

Stay calm, immobilize at heart level, mark swelling every 15min. DO NOT cut/suck/ice. Get antivenin.

Allergy/Anaphylaxis

Hives, swelling, breathing trouble. EpiPen if available. Position: sit if breathing issues, lie flat if fainting. Evacuate.

Pressure & Wind Signs

  • Wind backing (CCW) = worsening. Veering (CW) = improving.
  • Campfire smoke swirling/descending = low pressure/storm.
  • Aching joints, louder frogs, swallows low = rain coming.
  • "Red sky at night" = fair weather. "Red sky morning" = storm warning.

Optimal Tilt: `|latitude - declination|` (clamped 0-90°)
Real-world: 70-85% of rated wattage due to angle/temp losses.

Practical Tips

  • Clean daily in dusty conditions. 10% off angle = 5-15% loss.
  • Partial shade kills output. One cell blocked = whole panel drops.
  • Morning frost: Wait to melt. Don't wipe (scratches coating).
  • Series = higher voltage. Parallel = higher current. Match controller.
