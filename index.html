The Survival Mindset

Panic kills more people than the wilderness. Your mind is your most critical tool.

The STOP Protocol

Sit/Stop → Think → Observe → Plan.

Rule of 3s

  • 3 minutes without air
  • 3 hours without shelter (harsh conditions)
  • 3 days without water
  • 3 weeks without food

The 5 C's of Survival

These five hard items multiply your skills. Pack them first, before anything else.

C1 — Cutting Tool

Full-tang fixed blade knife (3-5 inches). The tang extends the full length of the handle. Never rely on folding knives for survival.

C2 — Combustion

Always carry at least 2 fire sources. Ferro rod is most reliable in wet conditions. Char cloth catches sparks instantly.

C3 — Cover (Shelter)

Debris hut for cold/wet. Lean-to for warmer conditions with fire. Dead air space = insulation. Two feet of debris can keep you warm below freezing.

C4 — Container

Single-wall metal bottle is non-negotiable. Boils water, cooks food, carries water. Double-wall insulated bottles will explode if heated.

C5 — Cordage & Knots

550 paracord (550lb test). 7 inner strands can be separated for fishing line, thread, or snares. Master the bowline and taut-line hitch first.

Finding & Purifying Water

Dehydration hits fast. Follow valleys downhill. Watch for converging animal trails, green vegetation, low-flying insects.

Source Safety Ranking

  1. Rainwater — Safest, collect with tarp/poncho
  2. Spring source — Flowing from ground
  3. Fast-moving stream — Clear, cold, over rocks
  4. Still water — Highest risk, purify thoroughly

DIY Water Filter (Plastic Bottle)

Need: 2-4 liters per day. Layer from finest to coarsest. Always purify after filtering — filtration alone does not remove viruses.

Transpiration Bag

Clear plastic bag tied over leafy branch. Sun heats leaves, water evaporates, condenses on bag, drips to corner. Use only non-toxic vegetation.

Wilderness First Aid

Field triage only. Get to professional help as soon as possible.

ABCDE Assessment

Airway → Breathing → Circulation → Disability → Exposure

🩸 Bleeding

  1. Direct pressure (5-10 min)
  2. Pack deep wounds with gauze
  3. Tourniquet: life-threatening limb only. Note time applied

🦴 Fractures

Splint joint above & below break. Open fracture: cover wound, do not push bone back. RICE for sprains.

🥶 Hypothermia

Remove wet layers. Rewarm core first. Never warm extremities first — causes cold blood rush to heart.

🥵 Heat Stroke

Hot/dry skin + confusion. Aggressive cooling: wet cloth, fan, ice to neck/armpits/groin. Evacuate immediately.

🐍 Snake Bite

Immobilize limb below heart. Mark swelling edge every 15 minutes. DO NOT cut, suck, or apply ice.

💉 Allergy

Hives, swelling, breathing trouble. EpiPen if available. Sit up if breathing, lie down if faint. Evacuate.

Reading the Sky

30-30 Lightning Rule: Flash-to-thunder ≤30 seconds? Lightning is within 6 miles — shelter immediately. Wait 30 minutes after last thunder before leaving shelter.

Cloud Identification Guide

Cumulus
Fair weather. Puffy, flat bottoms. Expect good conditions.
Cumulonimbus
Thunderstorm. Towering, anvil top. Seek shelter immediately.
Cirrus
High ice clouds. Weather change within 24-48 hours.
Stratus
Overcast layer. Drizzle possible. Stable but gray.
Altocumulus
Mid-level. Possible afternoon thunderstorms developing.

Wind rule: Backing (turns counter-clockwise) = weather worsening. Veering (clockwise) = improving. Heavy dew + clear night = fair weather continues.

Critical Solar Tips

  • Clean daily — dust reduces output 20-50%
  • Avoid any shade — even one shaded cell kills the whole panel
  • 100W panel = 70-85W in real-world conditions
  • Winter: tilt +15° steeper. Summer: tilt -15° flatter
  • Point toward equator: South (Northern Hemisphere), North (Southern)
