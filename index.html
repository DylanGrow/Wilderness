The Survival Mindset

Panic kills more people than the wilderness. Your mind is your most critical tool.

The STOP Protocol

Sit/Stop → Think → Observe → Plan.

Rule of 3s

  • 3 minutes without air
  • 3 hours without shelter (harsh)
  • 3 days without water
  • 3 weeks without food

The 5 C's

Hard items that multiply your skills. Pack first.

C1 — Cutting

Full-tang fixed blade (3-5"). Shelter, food, fire.

C2 — Combustion

Lighter ×2, ferro rod, matches. Char cloth, fatwood.

C3 — Cover

Debris hut, lean-to, quinzhee. Dead air = warmth.

C4 — Container

Single-wall metal bottle. Boil, cook, carry.

C5 — Cordage & Knots

Finding & Purifying Water

Dehydration hits fast. Follow valleys downhill. Watch for converging animal trails, green vegetation, low insects.

Source Safety Ranking

  1. Rainwater
  2. Spring source
  3. Fast stream
  4. Still water (highest risk)

Need: 2-4L/day. Layer improvised filters: grass → sand → charcoal → gravel. Always purify.

Wilderness First Aid

Field triage only. Get to professional help ASAP.

ABCDE

Airway → Breathing → Circulation → Disability → Exposure

Bleeding

  1. Direct pressure (5-10 min)
  2. Pack deep wounds
  3. Tourniquet: life-threatening limb. Note time.

Fractures

Splint joint above & below. Open fracture: cover, don't push bone. RICE for sprains.

Hypothermia

Remove wet layers. Rewarm core first. Never extremities first.

Heat Stroke

Hot/dry + confusion. Aggressive cooling: wet, fan, ice to neck/armpits/groin. Evacuate.

Snake Bite

Immobilize, mark swelling every 15m. DO NOT cut/suck/ice.

Allergy

Hives, breathing trouble. EpiPen if available. Position: sit if breathing, lie if faint. Evacuate.

Reading the Sky

30-30 Lightning Rule: Flash-to-thunder ≤30s? Shelter immediately. Wait 30min after last thunder.
